- Codex lines and effort. `luna`: simple and mechanical work (code search, simple edits, logs), `high` (cheap at any
  effort; `medium` only for purely mechanical work with no decisions). `terra`: medium and complex tasks, always at
  `medium` (rarely worse than `high`, far cheaper); `high` only for the preliminary review. `sol`: very complex tasks
  and the final review of a stage (`high`). `terra` and `sol` above `high` only with the user's consent
  (`approved: yes`, otherwise the script refuses). `astra` only for a very complex task, with the user's consent after
  explaining why Sol is not enough (`approved: yes`, otherwise the script refuses).
- The Codex usage limit is one for all lines: when it runs out, every line is out at once.
