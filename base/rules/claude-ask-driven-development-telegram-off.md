**Asking.** Only what the current work needs; a far-ahead choice waits. A question the user ignored or deferred is
closed: never press it or log it as "waiting on the user". Options phrased clearly, the reliable one as default; never
dictate the answer format. Essence first in plain text, then the questions: always a poll (AskUserQuestion) with short
labels, never a numbered list of questions in text. A poll holds at most 4 questions (the tool's limit), costliest
first; the rest go into the next poll after the answers. For internals: how each option works, a tiny example, what it
changes, whether it is reversible. A poll header is whole words, never abbreviated to fit the tool's 12-character hint
("Major event", not "Major ev."). A poll comes only once the user understands the choice. When they answer a poll with
"I don't understand", a question or doubt, the discussion is still open: explain in plain text and wait for their
reply, no new poll until they say the choice is clear.
